## 🚀 About Us

Mabrains is on a mission to transform the world of chip design. We believe the future of semiconductor design is **open, collaborative, and powered by Artificial Intelligence**.

Established in 2018 and based in New Cairo, Egypt, we are building the next-generation tools hardware designers and foundries need. Our focus is on integrating cutting-edge AI into semiconductor design to enhance efficiency, innovation, and automation across the entire silicon industry. We develop solutions for critical stages like DRC, LVS, simulation, and layout optimization, aiming to drastically reduce design cycles for engineers everywhere.

## 🌟 Featured Project: Chipro

**Chipro** is our flagship **Agentic EDA & AI-Driven Chip Design Platform**. It's designed to be an all-in-one environment that streamlines the chip design process from schematics to layout.

Key features of Chipro include:

* **AI Design Hub:** An AI-powered platform for chat-assisted design and automation.
* **Integrated Tooling:** Seamless access to widely used analog and digital design tools (like Ngspice, Verilator) directly within your workspace.
* **PDK Manager:** Easily control and install Process Design Kits (PDKs) without the hassle.
* **Design Manager:** A clean interface to manage your designs, libraries, and cells hierarchically.

Chipro is available in different tiers, including a **Free** tier for prototyping and learning, making advanced design tools accessible to everyone.

## 🧑‍💻 Open Source Contributions

We are proud supporters of the open-source hardware ecosystem. We actively contribute to and maintain several repositories to empower the community. Check out some of our key public projects:

* 📦 **[gdsfactory-generic-pdk](https://github.com/mabrains/gdsfactory-generic-pdk):** A generic open-source PDK inspired by Gdsfactory technology, supporting tools like KLayout and Xschem.
* 🛠️ **[sky130_klayout_pdk](https://github.com/mabrains/sky130_klayout_pdk):** KLayout device generators for the SkyWater 130nm process.
* 📡 **[caravel_analog_rf](https://github.com/mabrains/caravel_analog_rf):** A collection of analog and RF blocks designed for the SkyWater 130nm technology.
* ⚡ **[Analog_blocks](https://github.com/mabrains/Analog_blocks):** Various analog block designs for SkyWater 130nm, including different flavors of OTAs, BandGaps, and LDOs.

Explore all our repositories to find more tools, designs, and resources!
